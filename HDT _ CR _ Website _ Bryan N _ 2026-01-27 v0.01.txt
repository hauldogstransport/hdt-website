Executive Summary
This change request provides a comprehensive revision plan for hauldogstransport.com to align with your short-term business strategy: positioning the $75 KC metro auction pickup as the core value proposition. The current site does not mention the $75 price anywhere, lacks a clear service radius definition, and fails to prominently feature "loading help" and "no storage fees" as differentiators.
This document covers all four main sections (Process, Why Choose Us, Pricing, Get Started) plus the Hero section, with specific copy recommendations, structural changes, and UX improvements optimized for QR code traffic from business cards.
________________


Current Site Audit Summary
What Works Well
* Clean, professional design with good visual hierarchy and orange brand color
* Google Form integration for low-friction lead capture
* Process section presents a logical 3-step flow
* Mobile-friendly layout that should work well for QR scan traffic
* Stripe payment mention builds trust and signals modern payment processing
* Insurance focus appropriately targets auction buyers
Critical Gaps (Relative to Strategy Goals)
1. No $75 price mentioned anywhere on the site – biggest miss for the new strategy
2. No visual KC metro radius map or geographic service area definition
3. "Loading help included" is buried in generic copy, not called out as a differentiator
4. "No storage fees" appears once in a long bullet list, not emphasized
5. Vague "Fixed Pricing" messaging without actual dollar amounts weakens value prop
6. No auction selector or dropdown – users must type free-form addresses
7. Out-of-radius pricing is completely unaddressed – no guidance for Odessa or farther locations
8. Process Step 3 ("Pickup from HDT") is confusing – reads like HDT picks up rather than customer picks up from HDT
